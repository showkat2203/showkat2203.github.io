## Md Showkat Hossain Chy

Builds systems where correctness under concurrency, failure, and scale is the
hard part — MCU firmware under fixed memory and power budgets, distributed
services that reconcile out-of-order events, and machine learning over graphs
with millions of nodes.

Seattle, Washington.

**Now** — founder and engineer at an early-stage AI startup, building an
agentic platform that monitors municipal records, council agendas, zoning
dockets, and permit filings across U.S. cities and converts them into early
business signals for commercial real estate and multi-site operators.

**Before**

- **Amazon Web Services**, software development engineer, AWS infrastructure
  supply chain (2025–2026). Modelled millions of hardware components and bills
  of materials as a graph in Neptune and DynamoDB, and held the system of record
  to a 24-hour consistency commitment with an event-driven reconciliation
  service that replayed dropped, duplicated, and out-of-order updates.
- **CloudHubs Lab, University of Arizona**, research associate II (2024). Led
  the lab's graph-learning line: graph neural networks and variational
  autoencoders over static-analysis dependency graphs, published at IEEE/ACM ASE
  and in Cluster Computing.
- **Samsung Research**, software engineer, wearable platform (2018–2021).
  FreeRTOS firmware and drivers in C and C++, the Bluetooth Low Energy and GATT
  control and data planes on a 15 ms cadence, and rollback-safe over-the-air
  updates for wearables shipped to more than two million devices.

**Research** — 12 peer-reviewed publications, 148 citations, h-index 8, in
IEEE/ACM ASE, ECSA, CLOSER, Journal of Systems and Software, Cluster Computing,
Electronics, and Telecom.
